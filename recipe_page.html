Homemade Tea

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water


  • 2 tsp black tea leaves


  • 1 cup milk


  • 2 tsp sugar (optional)

  • 1-2 cardamom pods (optional)


  • 1 small piece of ginger (optional)


  • 1 cinnamon stick (optional)


  • 1-2 cloves (optional)


  • 1-2 black peppercorns (optional)

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a pot.
  2. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  3. Reduce heat and let it simmer for 5 minutes.

Cooking Tips

  • Adjust the amount of sugar according to your taste.
  • For a stronger flavor, let the tea simmer for a few more minutes.
  • You can strain the tea before serving for a smoother texture.

Serving Suggestions

  • Serve hot with a slice of lemon or a sprig of mint.
  • Pair with your favorite biscuits or cookies for a delightful snack.

Nutrition Information (per serving)

  • Calories: 50
  • Carbohydrates: 10g
  • Protein: 1g
  • Fat: 1g

Additional Notes

  • You can customize the recipe by adding your favorite spices or herbs.
  • Store any leftover tea in the refrigerator and reheat before serving.
