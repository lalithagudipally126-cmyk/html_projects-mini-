Movie Information

Title: Moana

Genre: Adventure/Comedy

About the Movie

Moana is a young Polynesian girl who dreams of exploring the ocean and discovering her true purpose. Along her journey, she meets the demigod Maui and together they face numerous challenges to save their people and restore the heart of Te Fiti.

Main Characters

  • Moana: The brave and determined protagonist who embarks on a journey to save her people.

  • Maui: A charismatic demigod with a troubled past, who becomes Moana's unlikely ally.

  • Granma Tala: Moana's wise and nurturing grandmother, who shares the story of the heart of Te Fiti.
